# Sources and guarantee scope

Research checked **2026-10-30**. Match deployed broker/client/database versions and actual persistence, replication, retention and acknowledgment configuration. Product defaults and SDK retries are not universal guarantees.

- [RabbitMQ confirms and acknowledgments](https://www.rabbitmq.com/docs/confirms): separate publisher/broker and broker/consumer handoffs, channel-scoped delivery tags, confirmation timing and confirms for unroutable messages. This is documented product behavior, not end-to-end processing assurance.
- [Redis Pub/Sub](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/use-cases/pub-sub/): ephemeral at-most-once delivery and loss for disconnected subscribers. This official page was used after the older `/develop/pubsub/` endpoint timed out.
- [Redis Streams](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/data-types/streams/): retained entries, consumer groups, acknowledgment and pending/reclaim behavior. Persistence, failover and trimming still determine what can be recovered.
- [Redis distributed locks](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/clients/patterns/distributed-locks/): ownership/expiry assumptions and fencing guidance. A lock pattern is not proof that an external resource rejects stale writers.
- [Apache Kafka versioned documentation](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/) and [Confluent delivery semantics](https://docs.confluent.io/kafka/design/delivery-semantics.html): producer/consumer/transaction scope and external-system limitations. The release catalog includes 4.4; this skill does not prescribe its configuration. An attempted 4.4 design-page fetch was unavailable, so no version-specific 4.4 feature claim is made.
- [AWS transactional outbox](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/transactional-outbox.html): database/event dual-write gap and duplicate publication. Its cloud sample and saga advice are options, not required infrastructure.
- [AWS timeouts, retries and jitter](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/): partial failure, unsafe repeats, resource budgets and retry amplification. This older first-party engineering account informs tradeoffs; choose actual project limits rather than copying fleet-specific numbers.
- [Google SRE overload guidance](https://sre.google/sre-book/handling-overload/): resource capacity, bounded work and load handling; large-fleet mechanisms are not mandatory for a small service.
- [CloudEvents 1.0.2 specification](https://github.com/cloudevents/spec/blob/v1.0.2/cloudevents/spec.md): a versioned interoperable envelope; its `specversion` is `1.0`, and it does not provide delivery, deduplication or authorization.
- [OpenTelemetry tracing API](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/otel/trace/api/) and [messaging spans](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/messaging/messaging-spans/): context, tracing relationships and conventions. The consulted messaging page is marked Development; preserve compatible installed instrumentation rather than forcing schema updates.

The installed skill-creator/UI guidance, [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification), [OpenAI skills guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-skills) and [Astra guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra) support independent discovery and progressive disclosure. [Skill evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills) does not turn structural validation into executed failure/recovery evidence.
